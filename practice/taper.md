# The Last 7 Days

The taper principle, in one line: **storage strength doesn't build in 5 days,
but retrieval strength does — so spend the final week making what you know
accessible, not adding things you'll half-know.** New material learned now
arrives at the interview with high familiarity and low retrievability — the
worst combination, because it *feels* prepared and isn't. The queue, the
flashcards, and the mixed sets are exactly the retrieval-strength tools; the
modules are the storage-strength tools, and their window has closed.

## D-7 to D-3 — mixed retrieval + final mocks

- **Zero new material.** Not "less" — zero. If a mock exposes a real gap,
  re-derive that one item from its module notes and queue it; do not "quickly
  cover" a module you skipped.
- Daily: `review.py due` sweep + one flashcard deck pass (10 min, out loud).
- One mixed set (`mixed_sets.md`) or one module-8 drill per day, full time box.
- The last 3–5 mocks land here (`mock_protocol.md`), the final one no later
  than D-3 — feedback needs a day to train and a day to settle.

## D-2 to D-1 — flashcards and checklists only

- Flashcard passes and checklists. No problems, no drills, no mocks: a miss
  now can't be retrained in time, so it buys anxiety and nothing else.
- D-1: skim `ml/06_ml_systems/checklists.md` and your own invariant note cards
  (the dsa week-3 cards — invariants, not solutions). Reading, not solving.
- **Normal bedtime, both nights. Nothing after dinner.** The sleep evidence,
  honestly stated: sleep is when the hippocampus replays and consolidates the
  day's retrieval practice into durable memory, so cramming that costs sleep
  subtracts from exactly the process it's trying to feed; and one night of
  restriction measurably degrades next-day working memory and attention — the
  two resources an interview taxes hardest.

## Morning of

- One already-solved easy warm-up (a folder-01 problem you've passed twice) —
  to start the engine, not to learn. Solved in 10 minutes, ego intact.
- Read your invariant note card once.
- **Nothing new.** A new problem the morning-of has two outcomes: you solve it
  (learned nothing, spent focus) or you miss it (spent confidence). Both lose.
