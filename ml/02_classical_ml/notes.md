# Classical ML from first principles

Read in this order:

1. [Linear models](01_linear_models.md) (§1 to §3): linear regression, the
   normal equation and gradient descent, logistic regression and its
   gradient, ridge and lasso.
2. [Other classifiers](02_svm_knn_naive_bayes.md) (§4 to §6): SVMs and the
   kernel trick, kNN and the curse of dimensionality, naive Bayes.
3. [Trees and ensembles](03_trees_and_ensembles.md) (§7, §8): decision trees,
   bagging, random forests, gradient boosting, XGBoost.
4. [Clustering and dimensionality reduction](04_clustering_and_dim_reduction.md)
   (§9 to §12): k-means, PCA, Gaussian mixtures and EM, t-SNE and
   UMAP.
5. [Recommenders](05_recommenders.md) (§13): neighborhood methods, matrix
   factorization, ALS, implicit feedback, cold start.
6. [Interview questions & exit checklist](06_interview_questions.md): 26
   short model answers and the checklist to clear before the next module.

Then work through the notebooks and finish with [exercises.md](exercises.md).
